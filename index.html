Your study desk · help & backup

New HTML quizzes in the main folder of hijawwad/random-min-qui are checked automatically when this page opens. Use Refresh list after uploading. If the connection fails, the included or previously saved list stays available.

Subjects, months and weeks are inferred from filenames. Use search for ambiguous titles. A combined weekly quiz stays one card; choose its week inside the quiz.

Stars, completion labels and recently opened quizzes are saved in this browser only. “Open again” reopens a quiz; answer-level resume depends on that quiz’s own save feature. This homepage does not change quiz questions or scores.
